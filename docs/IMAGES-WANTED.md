# Cars still needing a photo

50 of the 661 cars have no photo. In the game they show as a plain
text box instead of a picture. That works, but it looks unfinished.

**Every car here has already been searched automatically and come up short.**
40 turned up a photo that was wrong and got thrown out. The other 10 turned
up nothing at all. These need a person to go look.

Nothing here is older than 1974 — cars that stopped being made before then
are no longer part of the game at all, so they do not need photos.

## Where to look

**Wikimedia Commons, and only there** — https://commons.wikimedia.org

This is not a preference, it is a legal limit. Commons only accepts pictures
that are free for anyone to reuse, so anything hosted there can go on the site
as long as the photographer is credited — which the credits page does
automatically. A photo from anywhere else (Google Images, a car maker press
site, an auction listing, a forum) is almost always still owned by someone and
cannot be used.

**One catch.** A picture showing on an English Wikipedia article is not
necessarily on Commons. Some are uploaded to Wikipedia itself under rules that
do not allow reuse. Click the picture: if the page that opens is on
commons.wikimedia.org, it is fine. If it stays on en.wikipedia.org, skip it.

Two other good ways in, besides the search links below:

- Commons files cars into category pages, which usually beat the search box.
  Try `https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Mitsubishi_Starion`.
  Categories are often split by generation or year, which is exactly what the
  automatic search keeps getting wrong.
- A Wikipedia article about one specific generation nearly always has a photo,
  and that photo is usually on Commons. Click it to check.

## What makes a good one

- **The right generation**, not just the right name. By far the most common
  reason a photo gets thrown out.
- **The whole car, front or front corner.** Not an interior, an engine, or a
  close-up of a badge or tail light.
- **Hood and doors shut.** Car show photos very often have the hood up.
- **Normal paint.** No race numbers, sponsor stickers, flames or wraps.
- **Car filling most of the picture**, not a small shape in a big landscape.
- **The right body shape.** Each car lists the shape it is recorded as. That
  shape is used as a puzzle category, so a wagon photo on a car recorded as a
  sedan is a real mistake, not just an ugly one.

## How to hand one over

Copy the **exact file name** as Commons shows it at the top of the file page,
for example `1991 Mitsubishi Starion, front left.jpg`. Paste a list into the chat
and I will wire them up, or add them to `data/curation/pinned-files.json`
against the car id yourself. A name pinned there is used ahead of all the
automatic searching, so it always wins.
