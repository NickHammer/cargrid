# Flickr searches for the last photos

Every link below is already filtered to the licences Wikimedia Commons will
accept: CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0, Public Domain Mark, no-known-copyright and US
Government. Anything under a NonCommercial or NoDerivatives licence is excluded,
because Commons will not take it.

## Why this is a list and not finished work

A photo has to live on Commons before the game can use it. That is what lets
the pipeline check the licence and credit the photographer on the /credits
page, which the licences require. Moving a photo from Flickr to Commons has to
be done from your own Commons account, so I cannot do that step.

## Steps

1. Open a link. Pick a photo: front corner of the car, whole car in frame,
   standard car, and the right generation for the years shown.
2. Check it is really the photographer's own work. Some people put a free
   licence on photos they did not take, and Commons deletes those.
3. Send it to Commons with Flickypedia: https://flickypedia.flickr.org/
4. Copy the new Commons file name and paste it into
   data/curation/pinned-files.json against the car id, or just send me the list.

## Realistic odds

Four of these are probably not findable at all. The 1973 Skyline GT-R had 197
cars built. The 2025 Micra is too new for anyone to have photographed it
properly yet. The three Lancer Evolutions are rare Japanese-market cars, and
most photos of them are of modified or rally examples.

The best odds are the Mitsubishi Galant, the Nissan Patrol, the 1953 Cadillac
Eldorado and the Jeep CJ-6 — all ordinary classics with lots of enthusiast
photography.
